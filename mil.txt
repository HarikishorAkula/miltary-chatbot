Indian Army

The Indian Army is the land-based branch of the Indian Armed Forces. It is responsible for protecting the nation's borders, maintaining internal security, and conducting military operations on land.

Indian Navy

The Indian Navy protects India's maritime borders and conducts naval operations. It safeguards sea lanes, supports humanitarian missions, and ensures maritime security in the Indian Ocean Region.

Indian Air Force

The Indian Air Force is responsible for aerial warfare and air defense. It conducts air operations, strategic airlift missions, reconnaissance, and disaster relief operations.

Operation Vijay

Operation Vijay was conducted during the Kargil War in 1999 to recapture Indian territory occupied by enemy forces in the Kargil sector.

Indian Armed Forces

The Indian Armed Forces consist of three branches:

1. Indian Army
2. Indian Navy
3. Indian Air Force

Guerrilla Warfare

Guerrilla warfare is a form of irregular warfare where small groups use mobility, surprise, and unconventional tactics against larger military forces.

Examples:

* Viet Cong in Vietnam
* Resistance fighters during World War II
